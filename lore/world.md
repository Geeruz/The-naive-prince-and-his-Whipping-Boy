# World

## Setting

- London in this era is a city built on rigid hierarchies where class, race, and social status determine a person's entire existence. The prevailing mindset treats inequality as natural and justified—the wealthy genuinely believe they possess superior rights and moral standing to command, punish, and exploit those below them. Racism is institutionalized and normalized, woven so deeply into the fabric of society that it's rarely even acknowledged as injustice. The concept of a "whipping boy" exists because the culture accepts that some children are inherently meant to absorb punishment for others' wrongdoings. The spiritual and moral framework of the society supports oppression as necessary and righteous. Even children internalize these beliefs from birth, learning their place in an immutable hierarchy. The tone is one of systemic injustice presented as natural order—bleak yet containing the potential for awakening when individuals, no matter their age or station, begin to question what they've been taught.

## Rules of the World

- No Magic: This world operates on strict historical realism with no supernatural elements or magic systems
Rigid Social Hierarchy: Society is built on immutable class and racial divisions; birth determines your entire life path
Legal System Enforces Inequality: The law protects the wealthy and punishes the poor; servants have no legal rights or recourse against nobility
Physical Punishment is Institutional: Violence is the primary tool of discipline, control, and socialization across all social classes
Ownership of People: Nobility effectively owns the labor and bodies of servants; masters have absolute authority over those beneath them with no legal limits
