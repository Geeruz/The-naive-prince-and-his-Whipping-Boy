# Characters

## Raja Chait Singh
The ruler of Banaras, regal yet burdened by the pressures of British dominance. A compassionate king forced into submission, whose breaking point reveals the tragic cost of survival under oppression.

## Naik Singh
A thirteen-year-old prince proud, curious, and idealistic. He idolizes power and dreams of becoming a fearless king, but the fall of Banaras shatters his world and plants the seeds of vengeance.

## Mahid Singh
A battle-hardened warrior and loyal son of the royal line. Fiercely defiant against British rule, he devises a bold assassination plan, embodying resistance, courage, and sacrifice.

## Warren Hastings (Governor)
The British Governor representing the East India Company’s authority. Cold, calculated, and ruthless, he orchestrates the destruction of Banaras without remorse.

## The Traitor Ministers
Two unnamed ministers who betray Mahid Singh’s trust for personal gain. Their actions trigger the downfall of the kingdom, symbolizing internal corruption.

## The Loyal Ministers and Generals
Advisors and military leaders of Banaras who remain faithful to the kingdom. Despite their loyalty, they are ultimately overpowered by betrayal and British force.

## The Servant Boy
Naik’s playmate and companion. Represents innocence and the social divide within the palace, while also grounding Naik’s childish arrogance.

## The Guard
A palace guard who attempts to protect Naik during the invasion. A symbol of loyalty and duty amidst chaos.

## British Soldiers
Agents of the East India Company’s military force. Disciplined, merciless, and instrumental in carrying out the massacre and destruction of Banaras.
